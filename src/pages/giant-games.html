Questions about giant games

How much does it cost to rent a giant game?

Most giant and yard games are $50 each. Croquet and fowling are $60, giant snakes and ladders $75, golf pong $100 and giant chess $150.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Ottawa, Gatineau and surrounding areas, including Kanata, Orléans, Barrhaven and Nepean.

How big is the cornhole set?

Each cornhole board measures 4 ft by 2 ft and sets up on any flat area of lawn.

How do I book?

Send a quote request with your date, location and the games you want. The quote is free and there is no obligation.
