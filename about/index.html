About us

About Happy People Rentals

Fun and affordable event rentals in Ottawa, Gatineau and surrounding areas. Making every event memorable.

What we do

Happy People Rentals provides event rentals for birthdays, weddings, corporate events, school events, church events, community celebrations, festivals, family gatherings and backyard parties.

Our inventory includes yard games, giant games, party games, arcade games, inflatable games, a bouncy castle, a photo booth, flower walls, a flower bar, tables, chairs and canopies. Popular choices include Giant Connect 4, Giant Jenga, cornhole, Giant Guess Who, Giant Tetris, Plinko, ladder toss, Spikeball, Kan Jam, table tennis, foosball and arcade basketball.

Whether you're planning a small backyard celebration or a large community or corporate event, we make it easy to add interactive entertainment, seating and event essentials.
