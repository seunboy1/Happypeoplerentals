About Happy People Rentals

We rent giant yard games, arcade games, tables, chairs and canopies for events in Ottawa, Gatineau and the surrounding area, and we deliver, set up and pick up every order ourselves.

What we bring

Our inventory covers yard games, giant games, party and arcade games, plus tables, chairs and canopies. The favourites are Giant Connect 4, Giant Jenga, cornhole, Giant Guess Who, Giant Tetris, Plinko, mini golf, ladder toss, Spikeball, Kan Jam, bocce, table tennis, foosball and arcade basketball.

We set up for small backyard birthdays and for community and corporate events with hundreds of guests. The games work for kids, families, coworkers and guests of any age.
